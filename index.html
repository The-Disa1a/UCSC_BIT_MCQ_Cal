Start practicing See what it does

Free to use. Your MCQ answers stay in your browser. Gemini essay marking is optional and uses your own API key.

  1. 1Choose a paperBrowse semester papers or build a custom practice sheet.

  3. 3Check and improveReview MCQ scoring and optional essay marks with a breakdown.

What you can do

Everything in one practice space

Practice past papers

Select papers by semester and course, with PDFs alongside your questions.

Understand MCQ marks

Calculate scores using the paper's answer type and marking rules.

Try essay questions

Annotate the paper and optionally request Gemini marking against course material.

Make a custom sheet

Build and save your own MCQ papers for quizzes and focused revision.

Who it is for

Built for UCSC BIT learners

Use it for self-paced revision, timed mock exams, or checking how your answers compare with available keys. The project is community-maintained, so paper links and verified answers improve as students contribute.
